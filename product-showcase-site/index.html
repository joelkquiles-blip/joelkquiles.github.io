Yves Saint Laurent — Babycat

Discover the Scent

Babycat is an ode to warmth and raw sensuality. Opening with a spark of citrus and spice, it blooms into soft suede and iris before settling into its most seductive form — a rich, creamy vanilla bourbon that lingers on the skin for hours. Bold without being loud. Intimate without being soft.

Fragrance Profile

Explore the Notes

Top: Mandarin & Black Pepper — a bright, spiced opening that announces your arrival.

Heart: Suede & Iris — powdery, tactile, impossibly soft against the skin.

Base: Vanilla Bourbon — deep, sweet, and utterly addictive. This is where Babycat lives.
